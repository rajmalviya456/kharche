Kistt Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Kistt app ("Kistt", "the app") handles on Android and iOS, why, and the choices you have. Kistt is made by Raj Malviya, an individual developer ("I", "me").

Summary

  • Your loans, EMIs, payments, reminders and settings stay on your phone (and in your phone's own backup, if you use one). I never receive them.
  • Kistt has no accounts or sign-in.
  • Ads (Google AdMob) and purchases (RevenueCat, Google Play, App Store) are run by third parties that receive limited device and usage data, described below.
  • Kistt is meant for adults (18 and over).

Data that stays on your phone

Everything you enter (loan amounts, interest rates, tenures, start dates, paid instalments, reminders, preferences) is stored on your device. It is never sent to me.

If your phone backs up apps (Google backup on Android, iCloud or a computer backup on iPhone), that backup can include Kistt's data. It is kept in your own Google or Apple account, which I cannot read, and is restored if you reinstall Kistt. Uninstalling the app deletes the data from the phone; a backup in your own account stays until you remove it there.

Some features send data off the phone only when you choose to:

  • PDF / CSV export and backup: a file is created and handed to the app you pick (email, Drive, WhatsApp and so on). Where it goes from there is up to you and that app.
  • Share diagnostics: the app keeps a small error log on the phone. Nothing is sent automatically; if you tap Share diagnostics, you choose where the log goes.
  • Reminders and the home-screen widget run on the phone using the system's notification and widget services.

Your choices

  • Ad consent: where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), Kistt asks for consent before personalised ads. You can change your choice any time in Settings › Privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID (Android): reset or delete it in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Google › Ads).
  • Tracking (iOS): Kistt asks through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; you can change it in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Notifications: turn EMI reminders off in Kistt (My loans › EMI reminders, or the bell on a loan) or in the phone's notification settings.

Deleting your data

Data on your phone: uninstall the app, or clear its data in your phone's settings. Data in your phone's backup: delete it in your Google account (Android) or iCloud / computer backups (iPhone).

Purchase data held by RevenueCat: email me at raj.malviya456@gmail.com with your Google Play order number (starts with GPA.) or App Store receipt, and I will delete the matching RevenueCat record and confirm by email, usually within 30 days. Your purchase stays valid with Google or Apple and can be restored later.

Advertising data is controlled by Google; use the advertising ID controls above and Google's own tools at My Ad Center.

Retention

I do not store your data on any server of my own. Your phone's backup is kept by Google or Apple in your own account. RevenueCat keeps purchase records for as long as needed to provide Premium and meet legal and accounting duties, or until you ask me to delete them. Google keeps advertising data according to its own policies.

Children

Kistt is a finance tool for adults and is not directed at children under 18. I do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app and data was collected, contact me and I will help remove it.

Security

Data leaves the phone only over encrypted connections. Data on the phone is protected by your device's own security; keep your phone locked and updated.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the EU/UK GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, and to withdraw consent. Contact me to use these rights.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
